## Combat readability

The battle UI now derives display data from engine state instead of guessing monster intent.

Confirmed prepared Charge and Reactive states are presented prominently. Active effects show their remaining turns, stack counts and numerical modifier where applicable. Shields expose remaining capacity and authored maximum. The enemy information panel lists authored monster skills and cooldown state.

No new combat action type or AI condition was introduced for this release.

## Bestiary

The unified catalog currently exposes 45 canonical production-content entries:

- Iron Vein Spire: 10
- Red Fang Sanctuary: 10
- Celestial Crystal Tower: 25
- Kaleon Green Spire: 0

Progress is persisted by canonical monster id.

Unlock rules:

- encounter: identity / art / floor
- first normal defeat: skill names
- third normal defeat: full skill information
- first boss defeat: full information

Unknown QA definitions are ignored by persistent bestiary tracking.

## Save compatibility

v22 adds only the bestiary progress field.

v21 saves are migrated with an original-save backup. If a v21 save contains an active canonical encounter, that current encounter is preserved as one encounter entry during migration.

The original main save key remains unchanged.

## Content status

Playable through tower selection:

- Iron Vein Spire
- Celestial Crystal Tower

Red Fang content remains present in data but locked from normal tower selection.
Kaleon monster content remains unimplemented.

Crystal Tower dedicated monster sprites remain a follow-up asset task; current encounters use crystal guardian placeholder art.
